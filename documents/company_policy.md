# Global Enterprise Logistics & Sales Policy

## Section 1: Customer Refund and Return Policy
All return requests must be submitted within 30 days of the recorded delivery date. 
Returned products must be in their original packaging and unused condition. 
Custom-ordered or bulk wholesale freight orders exceeding $50,000 are subject to a standard 10% restocking fee. 
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days following inspection at our distribution warehouse.

## Section 2: Shipping, Freight, and Order Delivery
Orders placed through the Wholesale channel are fulfilled from regional warehouses within 2 to 4 business days. 
Distributor shipments require scheduled freight pickup with a minimum of 48 hours notice. 
Export orders require completed customs declaration documentation and typically require 10 to 18 business days for international transit.

## Section 3: Payment Terms and Volume Discounts
Standard commercial credit terms are Net 30 for qualified wholesale accounts. 
Accounts ordering over 500 units per calendar month qualify for an additional Tier-1 volume rebate of 3.5% applied directly to the invoice Line Total. 
Accounts that exceed 60 days past due will have credit privileges suspended until all balances are settled.
